
PRELIMINARY EMPIRICAL FINDINGS

The analysis examines the relationship between trade-flow
imbalance and subsequent short-horizon returns across
eight equities using minute-level observations.

The estimated market-impact coefficients range from
1.4590 to 3.5093 basis points.
All coefficients are positive: True.

Statistical inference using Newey-West HAC standard
errors indicates that all eight coefficients are
significant at the 5% level: True.

The regression R-squared values range from
0.0153 to 0.0431, suggesting that
trade-flow imbalance explains a limited proportion
of short-horizon return variation.

Out-of-sample directional prediction accuracy ranges
from 56.28% to 60.69%.
The average improvement over the majority-class
baseline is 7.46 percentage points.

These findings provide preliminary evidence of a
positive association between trade-flow imbalance
and subsequent returns. Further robustness analysis
and methodological review are required before making
stronger claims about market impact.
